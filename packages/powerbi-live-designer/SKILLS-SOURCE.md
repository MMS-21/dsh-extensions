# Skill source and local integration

The three dashboard-design skills are copied from MMS-21/agent-skills at revision `b00092451d9a7eac9a93197c8891a27ec8a5a4a5`, under its MIT license. The upstream reference material and attribution remain in those bundles. Local additions: the design-baseline reference, clarification of consequential business ambiguity, task-based exceptions for overflow and uncommon patterns, and evidence-based review outputs. `analysis-report-workflow` is the general integration skill; its Power BI reference describes only implemented adapter capabilities.

Dreamer remains the owner of reusable lessons and reviewed skill activation. This plugin records task operations and findings; its workflow skill routes optional learning proposals through Dreamer's existing tools. No automatic lesson acceptance, direct Dreamer storage edits, or extra model-provider calls are introduced.

Upstream author, version and compatibility fields are retained under metadata for the local skill validator; skill names, reference directories and attribution are preserved.
